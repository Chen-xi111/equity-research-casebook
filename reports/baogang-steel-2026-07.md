# Baogang Steel: cyclical repair without trend confirmation

**Security:** Inner Mongolia Baotou Steel Union (600010, Shanghai)

**Research date:** 27 July 2026

**Purpose:** Historical case study; not a current recommendation.

## Snapshot

The reviewed market snapshot placed the shares near CNY 2.20 after a rebound
from the early-July low around CNY 2.04. Short-term price behavior had improved,
but the stock remained close to a broader congestion area rather than in a
confirmed medium-term uptrend.

The useful distinction was between a tactical repair and a durable change of
regime. A close above roughly CNY 2.26–2.27 with stronger participation would
have provided better trend evidence. A return below roughly CNY 2.10, and
especially the earlier low, would have invalidated much of the repair thesis.

## Fundamental context

The company's 2026 interim profit announcement pointed to a year-on-year
improvement, while the sector backdrop remained mixed. Production discipline
could reduce supply pressure, but weak property-linked demand and uneven raw
material costs limited confidence in a broad steel-margin recovery. Rare-earth
economics and product-mix improvement were possible company-specific offsets.

## Scenario map

- **Confirmation:** sustained trade above the recent range with improving
  volume, followed by evidence that earnings quality and cash flow support the
  reported profit recovery.
- **Range continuation:** price holds above the early-July low but repeatedly
  fails near the short-term resistance band.
- **Invalidation:** a decisive close below the prior low while steel demand and
  margins remain weak.

The scenario map is deliberately position-agnostic. It describes observable
conditions and does not prescribe share quantities or account-level actions.

## Monitoring questions

1. Is the interim improvement driven by recurring operating profit or by
   temporary/non-operating items?
2. Do operating cash flow and underlying profit move in the same direction?
3. Does demand improve enough to offset coal, coke, and iron-ore pressures?
4. Does a price breakout persist for more than one session?
