# Methodology and publication boundary

## Analytical sequence

Each case study uses a compact evidence ladder:

1. **Dated observation:** identify the security, market, and snapshot date.
2. **Price regime:** distinguish trend confirmation, repair, range, and
   invalidation using observable conditions.
3. **Volatility context:** avoid treating an ordinary high-volatility move as
   decisive evidence by itself.
4. **Fundamental context:** connect public filings and catalysts to specific
   questions that future disclosures can answer.
5. **Scenario map:** express conclusions as conditional outcomes that can be
   falsified.
6. **Limitations:** state timing, sample, cost, and data-rights constraints.

This is a monitoring framework, not a valuation model or trading system. It
does not estimate intrinsic value or optimize portfolio weights.

## Fact, calculation, and interpretation

- **Fact:** a value or statement tied to a dated market observation or public
  filing.
- **Calculation:** a reproducible transformation such as a moving average,
  return, or volatility estimate.
- **Interpretation:** a conditional research judgment that may be wrong.

The public reports retain the distinction in prose and avoid presenting a
technical level as a certain forecast.

## Privacy transformation

The private source reports contained individualized portfolio context. The
public casebook was rebuilt from scratch instead of copying those exports.

Excluded material includes:

- position quantities and acquisition prices;
- account or portfolio values and allocation percentages;
- personalized buy/sell quantities;
- broker account data and credentials;
- raw generated HTML/JSON report artifacts;
- raw broker-data exports.

The publication guard provides a second line of defense by checking public
files for forbidden artifact types and high-risk phrases. Human review remains
mandatory because automated pattern matching cannot understand every context.

## Reproducibility limits

The repository preserves the analytical structure and public-source links, not
the proprietary or licensed market-data inputs. It is therefore reproducible
as a writing and review framework, but not as an exact price-history rerun.
That limitation is preferable to redistributing data or personal context that
does not belong in a public portfolio.
